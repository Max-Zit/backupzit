Monitoring, reports & calendar

See at a glance whether everything is protected, find out what happened on any day, and send regular reports to management or customers.

Dashboard

Agents online, number of jobs and storage targets, runs in progress, and runs, failures and warnings in the last 24 hours. Red or yellow cards need attention. Below is the most recent activity.

Activity

Every backup and restore, newest first. Click a run for details: what was backed up, statistics, errors per file, and the restore options. Runs that are in progress refresh automatically.

Calendar

Calendar shows a month at a time:

  • Past days show what actually ran, colored by result (green, yellow, red). Repeated runs of a job on one day are combined, e.g. 08:00 Office files ×6.
  • Future days show what the schedules will start (blue).
  • Next 7 days lists every upcoming run in order.

Filter by agent to see one machine's plan. Click an entry to open the job.

Reports

Reports summarize any period: today, yesterday, last 7/30/90 days, this or last week or month, or a custom date range — for all agents, one agent or one job.

  • Number of backups, success rate, successful / warning / failed runs, data read and uploaded, restores.
  • A chart of backups per day.
  • Per job: runs, success rate, last result, last good backup and protected size. A job with no good backup in the period is highlighted.
  • All failures and warnings with their messages.

Use CSV to open the runs in Excel, Print / PDF for a document, or Email this report.

Scheduled reports

Under Scheduled reports create reports that are emailed automatically when a period is complete:

  • Daily — every morning, about the previous day.
  • Weekly — every Monday, about the previous week.
  • Monthly — on the 1st, about the previous month (ideal for customers or management).

Each report can cover all agents, one agent or one job, and go to its own recipients. The CSV with all runs is attached. Email must be configured under Settings.
