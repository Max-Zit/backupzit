Monitoring, reports & calendar

See at a glance whether everything is protected, find out what happened on any day, and send regular reports to management or customers.

Dashboard

The cards show agents online, backup jobs, storage targets, queued and running runs, runs, failures and warnings in the last 24 hours, and suspicious backups to review (possible ransomware, see Ransomware protection). Green means fine, yellow needs a look, red needs action. Below is the most recent activity.

Activity

Every run, newest first. Click a run for details: what was processed, statistics, errors per file, and — for backups — the restore options. Runs in progress refresh automatically. Scheduled runs show a clock icon.

Calendar

Calendar shows a month at a time:

  • Past days show what actually ran — backups and copies — colored by result (green, yellow, red). Repeated runs of a job on one day are combined, e.g. 08:00 Office files ×6.
  • Future days show what the schedules will start (blue).
  • A day with many entries shows four and +N more; click it to see all of them, click again to collapse.
  • Next 7 days lists every upcoming run in order.

Filter by agent to see one machine's plan. Click an entry to open the job. Copy jobs that run "after each backup" appear on the days their source job ran.

Reports

Reports summarize any period: today, yesterday, last 7/30/90 days, this or last week or month, or a custom date range — for all agents, one agent or one job.

  • Number of backups, success rate, successful / warning / failed runs, data read and uploaded, restores, restore tests (and failed tests), suspicious backups.
  • A chart of backups per day.
  • Per job: runs, success rate, last result, last good backup, protected size, uploaded data and the result of the newest restore test. A job without a good backup in the period is highlighted.
  • All failures, warnings and suspicious backups with their messages.

Use CSV to open all runs in Excel (formula-safe), Print / PDF for a document, or Email this report. The same data is available to monitoring tools through the REST API (/api/v1/report).

Scheduled reports

Under Scheduled reports create reports that are emailed automatically when a period is complete:

  • Daily — every morning, about the previous day.
  • Weekly — every Monday, about the previous week.
  • Monthly — on the 1st, about the previous month (ideal for customers or management).

Choose the hour, the scope (all agents, one agent or one job) and recipients (empty = the notification recipients). The CSV with all runs is attached. Email must be configured under Settings → Email notifications. Creating and removing scheduled reports requires the Backup operator or Administrator role.
